How it works

Six steps from a first application to a stable future.

Here’s what happens from the day someone applies to the day they move on to their next job.

  1. Apply

    People experiencing homelessness apply to join the program, often after hearing about it from a friend or someone who’s already come through it. No résumé or spotless work history required—just a willingness to show up and try.

  2. Onboard

    The first month lays the groundwork for employment. Participants complete weekly training, check in regularly with our team, and start earning wages right away.

  3. Work

    During this first month, participants take on consistent, paid work: 10 hours a week contributing to a genealogical research effort alongside a lab of BYU economics students. Each day, our participants and students alike help build the complete family tree of everyone born from 1800 onward. Their tasks include merging duplicate records, adding missing individuals, cross-referencing information across databases, and closing gaps for people already on the tree.

  4. Employment

    After the first month, participants are hired as part-time employees. They keep building skills that carry well beyond this project: working with records and databases, using Excel and other everyday tools, professional writing, and keeping a consistent schedule.

  5. Stabilize

    Participants work remotely for a set period, earning steady pay. It’s a small foothold that unlocks a lot: even a small but consistent income helps secure housing, reliable phone access, hygiene items, and transportation.

  6. Move forward

    Our goal is not to keep participants in the program indefinitely, but to prepare them for opportunities beyond it. Through meaningful work experience, professional development, and assistance obtaining essential documentation, participants are equipped to pursue stable, higher-paying employment.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can join the program?

The program is for people experiencing homelessness in Utah County. You don’t need a résumé or a recent job to apply.

Do participants need their own computer?

No. The work is fully remote and can be done anywhere with internet access, including public libraries, university campuses, shelters, or even a mobile device.

Are participants paid?

Yes. Participants are paid from the start of onboarding. After the first month, they are hired as part-time employees.

How long do people stay in the program?

Participants work for a set period. The goal is not to keep anyone in the program indefinitely, but to prepare them for stable, higher-paying employment beyond it.

What does the work actually involve?

Alongside BYU economics students, participants help build the family tree of everyone born from 1800 onward: merging duplicate records, adding missing individuals, cross-referencing information across databases, and closing gaps for people already on the tree.

How can I refer someone?

Use our contact form and choose “Referring someone.” Caseworkers and shelter staff are welcome to reach out too.

Get in touch about the employment program, or support the work with a donation.
