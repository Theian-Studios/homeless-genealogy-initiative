Fund real jobs, real skills, and a path to stability for Utah’s homeless community.

We hire people experiencing homelessness in Utah County for paid, remote genealogy work with BYU’s Record Linking Lab. The job comes with weekly training and support toward steady, long-term employment.

How the job works

No résumé or spotless work history is required, just a willingness to show up and try. The job is built around the barriers people face, rather than requiring that they clear them first.

The six steps of the program

Hours
10 hours a week, on a schedule participants set themselves
Location
Fully remote: a public library, a campus, a shelter, or a phone all work
Pay
Paid wages from the start of onboarding, then part-time employment after the first month
Training
Weekly sessions on technology, workplace skills, and closing employment gaps
The work
Linking every person in the U.S. census since 1800 to the FamilySearch Family Tree

A lasting impact

The program creates value both for the people it employs and for the families who benefit from their work.

Long-term employment

Participants build meaningful work experience, develop transferable skills, and overcome barriers to employment through employment itself.

Family connections for future generations

Working with BYU’s Record Linking Lab, employees make a lasting contribution to historical and genealogical research.
