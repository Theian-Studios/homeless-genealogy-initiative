Fund real jobs, real skills, and a path to stability for Utah’s homeless community.

We hire people experiencing homelessness in Utah County for paid, remote genealogy work with BYU’s Record Linking Lab. The job comes with weekly training and support toward steady, long-term employment.

Donate Meet our participants

Hosted by Brigham Young University’s Department of Economics, in collaboration with the BYU Record Linking Lab. Serving Utah County since 2026.

The problem

No job without an address. No address without a job.

Employers expect references, reliable transportation, clean clothing, a permanent address, and a phone that works. For people experiencing homelessness, those requirements can close the door before an interview ever happens.

Where we come in

So we built a job that doesn’t ask for any of that.

Participants work remotely for 10 hours a week, on their own schedule, and are paid from day one. Along the way, they help reconnect other families with their history while they rebuild their own.

A job that meets people where they are

No résumé or spotless work history is required, just a willingness to show up and try. The job is designed around the barriers people face instead of requiring them to clear those barriers first.

The six steps of the program

Hours
10 hours a week, on a schedule participants set themselves
Location
Anywhere with internet: a public library, a campus, a shelter, or a phone
Pay
Paid from day one, then hired as a part-time employee after the first month
Training
Weekly sessions on technology and workplace skills
The work
Linking every person in the U.S. census since 1800 to the FamilySearch Family Tree
