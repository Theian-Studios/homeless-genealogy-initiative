Fund real jobs, real skills, and a path to stability for people experiencing homelessness in Utah County.

STEP is a paid, remote job with weekly training, built for people in Utah County who are working their way out of homelessness. It’s a first step toward steady, long-term employment.

Donate Meet our participants

Hosted by Brigham Young University’s Department of Economics, in collaboration with the BYU Record Linking Lab. Founded in 2026.

The problem

No job without an address. No address without a job.

Employers expect references, reliable transportation, clean clothing, a permanent address, and a phone that works. For people experiencing homelessness, those requirements can close the door before an interview ever happens.

Where we come in

So we built a job that doesn’t ask for any of that.

None of that is required. And while participants rebuild their own lives, they help reconnect other families with their history.

How the job works

How STEP works

Three steps to a lasting job.

Here’s the path from day one to the next job.

  1. 1

    Start working

    Participants earn a paycheck from day one, helping BYU’s Record Linking Lab connect census records to the FamilySearch Family Tree.

    • 10 hours a week, on their own schedule
    • From a library, shelter, campus, or phone
  2. 2

    Build skills and stability

    Weekly training builds technology and workplace skills, and after the first month participants are hired as part-time employees.

    • Steady pay toward housing, a phone, and transportation
    • Experience with records, Excel, and writing
  3. 3

    Move on to a lasting job

    The goal isn’t to keep anyone in the program. It’s to prepare each person for what comes next.

    • Help getting an ID and other essential documents
    • Ready for stable, higher-paying work
