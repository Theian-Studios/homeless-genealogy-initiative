Referring someone

Caseworkers, shelter staff, friends, and family are all welcome to refer someone. In your message, please include:

  • Your name, your organization if you have one, and the best way to reach you
  • The person’s first name and how we can contact them, directly or through you
  • A little about their situation: where they’re staying, whether they have a phone or regular internet access, and the kind of work they want
  • Whether they know about STEP and have agreed to be referred

Please leave out Social Security numbers, medical details, and other sensitive information. We’ll ask for what we need when we follow up.
